Questions

What is the difference between dimensional and channel letters?

Channel letters are hollow and lit from inside. Dimensional letters are solid cut-outs with no internal lighting, though they can be backlit or lit from outside.

Can they go indoors?

Yes. Many of our dimensional letters hang on interior walls, reception areas and feature walls.

Can you match my logo and font?

Yes. Send the logo file or a photo of it and we build the letters to match.

Will I see it first?

Yes. We place the letters on a photo of your wall and send you the mockup for free before you commit.
