Questions

What is involved in installing a monument sign?

Design, permit approval, structural and footing work, electrical connection if it is lit, then fabrication and installation. We coordinate the job so you deal with one team.

Do you handle permits for monument signs?

Yes. These signs usually need a permit with site and structural details, and we prepare and submit it.

Can you just change the tenant panels?

Yes. Send a photo of the existing sign and the new name or logo, and we quote the panel change.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. We show the sign on a photo of your site for free before you commit.
