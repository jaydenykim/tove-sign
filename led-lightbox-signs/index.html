LED and Lightbox Signs

LED and lightbox signs, built and installed in California

A lightbox or LED cabinet sign is a lit box with your name and logo on the face. It is a solid choice when you want a bright, durable sign at a sensible cost, or when an older sign needs to be brought up to date with efficient LED lighting.

Types we build

Single and double-faced cabinets

Wall-mounted or pole-mounted illuminated boxes, built to your size and branding.

Blade and projecting signs

Signs that stick out from the wall so people walking or driving along the street can see them.

LED retrofits

Replace old fluorescent tubes in an existing sign with LED modules. Brighter, more even light and lower power use.

Who this is for

Businesses that need a bright, durable sign, shopping center tenants who must follow a landlord's sign criteria, and owners of older signs that are dim or failing.

Questions

Can you convert my existing sign to LED?

In most cases, yes. Send a photo of the sign and we tell you whether a retrofit or a new face makes more sense.

Will I see the design first?

Yes. We put the sign on a photo of your building and send you the mockup for free before you commit.

Do you install and repair?

Yes. We install new signs and repair or relamp existing ones.

Do I need a permit?

For most illuminated signs, yes. We prepare and submit the permit paperwork for you.
