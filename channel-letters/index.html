Channel Letters

Channel letter signs for California businesses

Channel letters are the individually built, lit letters you see on storefronts, offices and plazas. We design them, get the permit, build them in our shop and install them. Before any of that, we show you the sign on a photo of your own building.

Types we build

Front-lit

Light shines through the face of each letter. The most common choice for retail and restaurants. Bright and easy to read at night.

Halo-lit (reverse)

Light glows on the wall behind each letter. A softer, premium look that works well on stucco, brick and stone.

Non-illuminated letters

Dimensional metal or acrylic letters with no lighting. Useful for interiors and for buildings with no power at the sign location.

Who this is for

Storefronts, restaurants, medical and dental offices, banks, plazas and office buildings that need to be seen from the street, day and night.

Questions

How do I see the sign before I order?

Text us a photo of your storefront. We place the channel letters on your building and send you the mockup. It is free and there is no obligation.

Do you handle the permit?

Yes. We prepare and submit the city sign permit as part of the job. Each city has its own sign code, so we check it before we design.

Does the landlord have to approve it?

Often, yes. Many centers have their own sign criteria. Send us the lease sign section or the landlord contact, and we design to it.

How much do channel letters cost?

It depends on the size, the number of letters, the lighting type and the install. Send a photo and what you have in mind, and we quote it clearly.
