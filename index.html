Questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. We design it ourselves from your photo. No charge, no obligation.

What do I need to send?

A photo of your storefront. If you have a logo or a size in mind, send that too, but a photo is enough to start.

Do you handle city permits?

Yes. We prepare and submit the permit paperwork as part of the job.

Which areas do you serve?

Anywhere in California. Our shop is in Santa Fe Springs and we take jobs statewide.

What kinds of signs do you make?

Channel letters, LED and lightbox signs, monument and pylon signs, foam and dimensional letters, window stickers and graphics, banners and vehicle decals, plus repairs and LED retrofits.
